Introducing dots

OpenAI launched dots at DevDay: always-on ChatGPT agents powered by GPT-6 Astra, with their own cloud computer and browser, learning from feedback, 24/7 goal-directed work, and connections to over 4,000 apps. Reach them in ChatGPT (desktop, web, mobile), Slack, and Teams, including voice calls. You can inspect the cloud computer and permit a dot to use your laptop. Rolling out to eligible Pro and Business Premium users (Pro excluded the EEA, Switzerland, and the UK at launch); Enterprise/Edu/Healthcare could try a beta when an admin enabled it.
